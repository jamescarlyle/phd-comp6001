## Executive Summary

TriboAI has launched a revolutionary Machinery Condition Monitoring System that combines advanced electrostatic sensing with machine learning to detect early signs of bearing wear. The technology addresses a critical industrial inefficiency: the premature replacement of functional bearings to avoid catastrophic failure. This innovation enables operators to safely extend asset life while reducing costs by 18–25% and supporting the transition to sustainable green lubricants.

---

## The Problem Statement

Conservative preventative maintenance practices have created a costly paradox: industry replaces functional bearings to prevent rare catastrophic failures, yet this strategy generates massive waste while limiting adoption of sustainable alternatives.

**Unnecessary Replacement & Risk**

Bearing failures can result in catastrophic consequences—the 2023 Ohio train derailment involved a defective wheel bearing that caused a fire affecting 38 freight trucks carrying hazardous materials. Yet this extreme risk drives operators toward premature, expensive replacement of still-functional components. Research indicates refurbished bearings can reach 90% of the lifespan of new bearings with just 10% of the energy and material costs—but only if identified at the right point in their lifecycle.

**Environmental and Adoption Barriers**

Oil-based lubricants used in conventional bearings pose serious contamination risks to groundwater and local ecosystems. While plant-derived "green" lubricants offer environmental benefits, machinery operators hesitate to adopt them for critical safety applications due to lack of real-time performance assurance. This creates a catch-22: genuine commitment to sustainability is constrained by performance uncertainty.

## The TriboAI Solution

### Technology Overview

TriboAI's system functions as a "smart skin" for critical machinery, using non-intrusive electrostatic sensors embedded directly into bearings to monitor tribological conditions in real-time. Unlike traditional vibration-based approaches that often detect faults only after damage has occurred, TriboAI's sensors detect the earliest disturbances in lubricant film breakdown and surface interactions.

The system integrates seamlessly into existing infrastructure, feeding data into an AI-driven analytics platform that uses machine learning-based outlier detection methods to identify abnormal events signifying potential failures and pinpoint specific failure modes with high precision.

**Key Technical Advantages:**
- Non-destructive and non-interruptive monitoring
- Early-stage wear detection through electrostatic sensing
- Proprietary machine learning algorithms for anomaly detection
- Self-powered sensors suitable for highly-regulated industries
- Secondary applications: detection of external environmental issues (e.g., rail track failure)

### Innovation Pedigree & Technology Readiness

The technology emerges from 30+ years of research by Professor Robert Wood in electrostatic sensor development and the world-renowned National Centre for Advanced Tribology at Southampton (nCATS). The research methodology builds on well-established non-destructive sensing practices aligned with ISO standards for machinery vibration monitoring (ISO 10816, ISO/DIS 20816-3).

TriboAI operates at Technology Readiness Level 6 ("Demonstrator"), having progressed through laboratory validation with controlled experiments, field trials on operating machinery under variable environmental conditions, and engagement with 100+ industrial partners during development.

## Impact and Benefits

### Economic Impact

**Cost Reduction:** Predictive maintenance approaches can deliver cost reductions of 18–25% compared to standard preventative approaches, with savings up to 40% over purely reactive maintenance strategies.

**Market Opportunity:** The global predictive maintenance market is projected to reach $60 billion by 2030, with tribometer-specific applications addressing a $7 million near-term market opportunity.

**Job Creation:** The project is anticipated to generate over 20 skilled jobs within three years, contributing to growth in the UK's clean-tech sector.

### Environmental and Sustainability Impact

**Waste Reduction:** By enabling more accurate monitoring, the system allows operators to keep bearings in service longer, dramatically reducing unnecessary bearing replacement and associated manufacturing waste.

**Green Lubricant Enabler:** Real-time monitoring data provides the assurance machinery operators need to transition confidently from fossil-fuel-based oils to sustainable plant-based alternatives, preventing groundwater contamination and reducing industrial carbon footprints.

**Net-Zero Support:** The technology is particularly vital for UK machinery supporting decarbonisation goals, including offshore wind turbines (where bearing replacement is dangerous, costly, and weather-dependent), renewable energy systems, and power generation infrastructure.

### Safety, Reliability, and Broader Benefits

**Reduced Catastrophic Failure Risk:** The system enables operators to understand failure boundaries with greater precision, allowing safe reduction of uncertainty, cost, and safety margins. Real-world applications include early detection of train derailment risks and potential failures in power generation systems.

**Extended Asset Life & Remanufacturing:** More accurate monitoring enables operators to keep bearings in service longer and identify those suitable for remanufacturing—a process capturing 90% of bearing lifespan at just 10% of manufacturing cost and energy.

**Secondary Detection Capabilities:** Sensors embedded in bearings can detect external environmental issues beyond tribological wear, such as rail track defects, providing infrastructure protection value beyond the primary maintenance application.

## Risk Management and Governance

### Key Risks Addressed

TriboAI has identified and implemented mitigations for:
- **Technical Risk:** Sensor calibration and environmental interference → Prior technology research and experience overcoming scale-up issues
- **Security Risk:** Cyber vulnerabilities in connected systems → Ongoing penetration testing protocols
- **Commercial Risk:** Development funding and market adoption → Multiple partner integrations to increase likelihood of success
- **Reputational Risk:** Performance failures in operational deployment → Rigorous testing protocols before commercial at-scale deployment; honesty with customers; effective governance oversight

### Governance Structure

- **University Oversight:** Research project managed by Faculty of Engineering; university accountability maintained during PoC phase
- **Advisory Board:** External technical and commercial guidance with periodic reassessment of risks and assumptions
- **Data Management:** FAIR principles (Findable, Accessible, Interoperable, Reusable) with GDPR compliance and non-proprietary format storage
- **Ethical Commitment:** Founding team commits to ethical conduct, open research, and inclusive approaches (senior faculty with established obligations to unbiased research)

The project has identified novel intellectual property in electrostatic sensing for early-stage wear detection. Patent searches have not identified specific prior art. The team is pursuing a tiered IP protection strategy:

- **Trademark:** Cross-class trademark protection for TriboAI brand and related goods/services
- **Commercial Licensing:** IP portfolio will be licensed to TriboAI Ltd. to support commercialisation
